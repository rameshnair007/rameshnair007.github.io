What I'm Working On

At Planet Labs, I lead the work on machine learning (ML) pipelines. A pipeline is a chain of automated steps that turns raw data into results. Ours analyze satellite images at very large scale. I focus on four things right now:

  • Building high-performance models that find objects in images (detection) and label every pixel (segmentation), for infrastructure and land use
  • Designing workflows that train models from few or imperfect labels (weak supervision), so we can improve models quickly
  • Combining different kinds of data in one model: images over time, different light wavelengths (spectral), and radar (SAR), which can see through clouds
  • Making pipelines cover the whole globe, with little delay between a new image and a result

I also work on making models easier to explain (interpretability). I work with other teams to deliver tools that scientists, governments, and humanitarian groups use.
